Tooltip Props

- children (required): one element/component that forwards aria-describedby.
- content (required): short descriptive, noninteractive React content.
- side: top (default) | bottom. Flips near viewport edges when needed.
- delay: hover delay in milliseconds (default 250). Focus opens immediately.
- disabled: disables the tooltip without disabling its child.
- Native wrapper span props, sx/style, class/className are supported.

Preserves existing child descriptions, event handlers, and accessible names.
Shows role=tooltip and connects it through aria-describedby while visible.
Escape dismisses help without moving focus. Hover/focus can keep it open, and
the pointer can move onto the tooltip. Pending timers/listeners are cleaned up.

Uses a light, airy themed fill, comfortable spacing, no border/shadow, and a
delicate 140ms fade in/out. Reduced motion skips the animation. Tooltip content
positions within the inline wrapper; ancestors should allow overflow.
