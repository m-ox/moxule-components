MenuButton Props

- open: expanded state (default false).
- onClick: supplied by the parent to toggle its controlled Drawer.
- aria-controls: ID of the associated Drawer.
- aria-label: defaults to Open menu / Close menu according to open.
- Supports Button props except children; the icon is supplied by MenuButton.

Renders a borderless button with a rounded hamburger/close icon, aria-expanded,
and aria-haspopup=dialog. The icon is decorative. Colors inherit from the
surrounding toolbar or page, with themed hover and visible keyboard focus.
